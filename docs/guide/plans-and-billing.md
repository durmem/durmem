# Plans and billing

<!-- loom-doc: slug=plans-and-billing; audience=hosted; section=guide -->
Use this page to see what the paid hosted plans cost, how the free trial works, how to cancel,
and what happens to your memories if a plan ends. It applies to the hosted service at durmem.ai.
A private server has its own arrangement with whoever runs it.

## What each plan includes

| Plan | Price | Storage | Stores |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | 200 MB | 5 |
| Pro | $20 a month | 1 GB | 25 |
| Pro Plus | $50 a month | 5 GB | 100 |

Prices are in US dollars. If we are required to collect sales tax where you are, it is added at
checkout, so you see the total before you pay. A paid plan renews every month until you cancel.
Payments are handled by Stripe; Durmem never sees your card number.

## Free trial

Your organization's first Pro or Pro Plus plan starts with a 30-day free trial. You enter a card at
checkout, and you are not charged until the trial ends. The 30 days count from checkout. Your paid plan,
with its larger storage, starts when you click Switch now on your billing page, so switch soon to use all
of the trial; every day you wait before switching is a day of the trial you do not use. Once you have
switched, your account page says that your free trial lasts until its end date. Cancel from the billing
portal before the trial ends and you are never charged. If the trial ends without a card on file,
the plan ends, after up to three more days of access.

Each organization gets one free trial. If your organization has had a paid plan before, checkout says
"You have already used your free trial; you will be charged today."

## Starting a checkout

Only one checkout can be open for your organization at a time. If you started one and did not finish it (or
cancelled it), the billing page says "A checkout for your organization was started a moment ago. You can start
another in N minutes." After that time you can start a new one.

## Cancel

Cancel from the billing portal that your account's billing page links to. A cancellation takes
effect at the end of the month you have already paid for: the plan stays active until then, and
your account page shows "scheduled to cancel" with the date. After that date the account returns
to the Free plan.

## Refunds

If you cancel within 30 days of your first payment, we refund that payment in full. Later payments
are not refunded. To avoid a charge, cancel before your trial ends. After that you can cancel at
any time to stop the next payment.

## If a plan ends or a payment fails

When a paid plan ends, the plan keeps working for three more days. After that your memories stay.
The account keeps everything it has saved and your agents can still read it. If you hold more than
the Free plan allows, new saves are refused until you are back under the Free limits or you
subscribe again.

If a monthly payment fails, paid access continues for three more days while the payment is
retried. If it still has not gone through, the plan ends as described above.

## Get help

Paid plans include a support page at durmem.ai/support. Sign in and send your question there. For
problems with the product, open an issue at github.com/durmem/durmem/issues.
